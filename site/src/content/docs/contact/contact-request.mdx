This page helps you ask for a private conversation with the AID maintainer, for example about adopting AID in your team.

Write what you want to talk about in the form below; the message is required. The "Open your email app" button starts an email to the maintainer with your text filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it from your email app, so no public issue is created.

If you prefer a public thread, use [Ask or give feedback](/contact/feedback/).
