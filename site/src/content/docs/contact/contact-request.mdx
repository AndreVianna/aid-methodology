This page helps you ask for a private conversation with the AID maintainer, for example about adopting AID in your team.

The "Open your email app" link opens your email app with the subject already filled in; you write your message there. Nothing is sent until you send it from your email app, so no public issue is created.

If you prefer a public thread, use [Ask or give feedback](/contact/feedback/).
