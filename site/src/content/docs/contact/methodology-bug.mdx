This page helps you report a bug in AID itself, such as a problem in the `aid` CLI, a skill, an agent or a script.

Fill in the form below with a summary and what happened. Say what you ran and what you expected. Add the AID version and your environment if you know them, for example the AI tool and the operating system.

The "Continue on GitHub" button opens the new-issue page of the AID repository on GitHub, labelled "bug", with your text filled in. No field in this form is required.
