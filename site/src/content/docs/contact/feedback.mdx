This page helps you ask a question or share feedback or a suggestion about AID.

Write a subject and your message in the form below. The form opens a prefilled issue in the AID repository on GitHub, in a new tab, labelled "feedback". You submit it there, with your own GitHub account. The issue is public, so others can follow it.

For a private message instead, use [Request a conversation](/contact/contact-request/). Your name and email are optional, and an email you give appears in the public issue.
