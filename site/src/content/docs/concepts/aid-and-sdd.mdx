This page shows you how AID relates to spec-driven development, so you can see what AID adds around the spec.

[Spec-driven development](/reference/glossary/#sdd) (SDD) is a method where specifications drive code generation. AID (AI Integrated Development) includes it: the spec-and-build part of the pipeline does the same job. AID adds the work that comes before and after that span.

<AidVsSdd />

## Where they meet

Both put a written specification at the centre of the build. In AID, `/aid-specify` writes the technical specification for each feature, and `/aid-execute` builds from the tasks that come out of it.

## Where AID goes further

SDD starts from a spec. AID rejects that starting point as incomplete, because it assumes you already understand the system. AID starts from understanding, then adds the following.

| Question | SDD | AID |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Where does the work start? | With a written spec. | On existing code, with `/aid-discover`, which builds the [Knowledge Base](/concepts/knowledge-base/). On a new project, with `/aid-describe`. |
| What happens when the spec turns out to be wrong? | You write the spec again from scratch. | A [feedback loop](/concepts/feedback-loops/) sends the finding to the phase that owns it. |

Beyond those two answers, AID adds these parts around the spec:

- **Requirements.** `/aid-describe` gathers them through an adaptive interview, and `/aid-define` splits them into features.
- **Planning in two levels.** `/aid-plan` groups features into deliveries. `/aid-detail` then breaks a delivery into tasks.
- **Graded review.** A reviewer grades the specification, the plan and the task list against a minimum grade. `/aid-execute` reviews each task and then the whole delivery. The [pipeline page](/concepts/pipeline/#execute-and-review) explains how.
- **Shipping and monitoring.** `/aid-deploy` and `/aid-monitor` are optional. Monitor routes bugs to `/aid-fix` and change requests to `/aid-triage`.
- **Small changes.** A [shortcut](/get-started/lite-path/) runs a lighter path for work whose kind you already know, and `/aid-triage` suggests the right entry when you are not sure.

The [pipeline page](/concepts/pipeline/) gives the order of the phases. The [agents page](/concepts/agents/) explains who does the work in each one.
