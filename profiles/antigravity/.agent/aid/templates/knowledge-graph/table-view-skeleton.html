Files, concepts and relationships

Three tables (task-034), each carrying a different thing this project's Knowledge Base records, rather than one table repeating a node's own properties on every relationship row that names it: Files, a collapsible tree of every document, source artifact and image, with its sections and facts nested under it; Concepts, every defined term, flat; and Relations, every recorded relationship between two of the above. Every row in the first two tables carries a Show checkbox — unchecking one hides it from these tables and from the interactive relationship graph, and ONLY from the view: it never edits relationships.md and never changes a coverage count, and the selection is remembered the next time either page is opened. This is the text equivalent of the graph, and it stays fully usable wherever that page's drawing surface cannot run — no WebGL context, no JavaScript-drawn canvas, nothing visual-only standing between a reader and the data. The Relations table's rows load a window at a time; the "Load more" button reaches every one of them.
